# Speakeasy listing draft

App name: Speakeasy

Publisher: Aspect Studio Software

Short description:

Speak naturally. Type with your voice, offline, using your usual keyboard.

Full description:

Turn what you say into text with Speakeasy. Its floating microphone works with
your existing keyboard, so you can dictate into messages, email, notes, and other
editable text boxes.

English speech is transcribed on your phone by default. The speech model is
included, so there is no transcription account, API key, or extra speech-model
download to set up. Offline dictation keeps your recording on your device.

MAKE WORDS FIT THE MOMENT

Choose a writing style for personal messages, work chats, email, and other apps.
Speakeasy tidies fillers and punctuation and fits text around your cursor. You can
say commands such as "new line", "new paragraph", and "scratch that".

STAY IN CONTROL

Tap the floating microphone to start. Tap Stop to transcribe. Capture continues
across screens until Stop. Stop & keep saves audio for retry on device. Choose a detailed recording
panel, a compact timer, or a minimal microphone indicator.

KEEP WORDS THAT COULD NOT BE INSERTED

If the text box changes or an app refuses insertion, use Recovered transcripts
to copy or insert your words yourself. Up to five saved transcripts are retained
for 24 hours. Copy text you need to keep longer.

OPTIONAL EXTRAS

The Speakeasy keyboard is available if you prefer a dedicated dictation keyboard.
Smart cleanup uses an additional model you can choose to download once; cleanup
then runs on your phone. Optional cloud transcription uploads finished recordings
to a provider you configure, after a separate disclosure and consent. That
provider's terms and charges apply.

PERMISSIONS AND PRIVACY

Speakeasy uses the microphone only when you start dictation. Floating dictation
uses Android Accessibility to detect the keyboard, read the active editable field
and cursor, choose a writing style, and insert dictated text. This may include
sensitive text. Editor context stays on your phone. Password fields identified by
Android are excluded. Speakeasy does not use Accessibility to send messages or
operate other controls. You can decline or withdraw this access and use the
optional keyboard instead.

Some apps do not support direct insertion. Use Copy or the optional keyboard when
needed. Speakeasy supports English dictation on ARM64 phones running Android 8 or
later. The included speech model makes the initial download larger than many
keyboard apps. Transcription speed depends on your phone.

No ads. No analytics SDK. No Speakeasy account required.
