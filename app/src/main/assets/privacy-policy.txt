Speakeasy privacy policy
Effective 29 October 2026

This policy covers the Speakeasy Android app from Aspect Studio Software,
with Google Play package net.aspectstudio.speakeasy. Earlier
sideloaded and development versions use package com.cragnet.wysawyg.
Privacy and support contact: info@aspectstudio.net.

On-device dictation

Speakeasy transcribes English speech on your phone by default using its bundled
speech model. Recordings are captured only when you start dictation. Stop finishes
the recording and transcribes it. Stop & keep ends capture without transcription
and retains the audio. Capture continues across keyboard and screen changes until
you stop it or Android interrupts it; there is no five-minute capture cutoff.
Speakeasy does not automatically switch to cloud when local transcription fails.

On-device dictation does not send your recording, transcript, or the text around
your cursor to us or a transcription provider. Audio is written during capture to
private app storage excluded from backup, and processed in bounded segments.
Interrupted or failed recordings and completed segment text remain on the phone
until the complete transcript is saved or you explicitly discard the audio.
One pending recording must be resolved before another starts. Home → Recovered
transcripts offers Retry on device and confirmed Discard audio. Recovery is never
uploaded automatically, even if cloud settings change. Force-stop can stop the
microphone; reopening retains the prefix already written without restarting it.

Accessibility and the optional keyboard

If you choose the floating microphone, Speakeasy asks for separate consent before
using Android Accessibility. It observes the active app and keyboard visibility
and reads the focused editable field, its text, and its cursor or selection. This
can include sensitive text such as private messages. It uses this information to
show the microphone, choose a writing style, preserve surrounding text, and insert
the words you dictate. Editor context stays on your phone and is not sent with
cloud requests. Password fields are excluded when Android identifies them as
password fields. Speakeasy does not use Accessibility to send messages or operate
other controls on your behalf.

The optional Speakeasy keyboard accesses the current editor through Android's
input method interface. It can insert dictated text without Accessibility access.
Android permissions for the microphone, overlays, and notifications are managed
in your device settings. When notifications are allowed, the ongoing notification
offers Stop during recording and Recover after interruption. Without overlay
permission, notifications must be enabled before starting through the optional
keyboard. Pause in Home prevents new floating starts while an active recording
continues until Stop. Starting the phone or resuming the service never starts a
recording.

Optional cloud transcription

Cloud transcription is off by default. If you configure a provider and agree to
the separate cloud disclosure, recordings you finish are sent to that provider
over HTTPS. The request includes your voice recording, model choice, API key if
configured, and a transcription prompt for providers that support it. The
provider returns the transcript. Your recording may contain personal information
you speak. Only choose a provider whose privacy and retention terms you accept.

The provider, rather than Speakeasy, controls how it processes and retains data
after receiving it. Speakeasy cannot promise a provider's deletion, training, or
retention practices. Contact that provider to request deletion of data it holds.
Speakeasy does not run its own transcription server. Changing to a different
provider origin requires consent for that destination. Imported settings select
on-device transcription and cannot grant consent.

Switch to On-device in More settings or select Turn off cloud in Privacy and
permissions to withdraw cloud consent for future requests. This cannot recall a
recording that a provider has already received or a request already in progress.

Optional Smart cleanup model

If you request Smart cleanup, Speakeasy downloads an additional model from Hugging
Face and its download infrastructure. Those services receive normal connection
information, including your IP address, when you download the file. Their privacy
policy is at https://huggingface.co/privacy. Cleanup then runs on your phone.
Speakeasy does not upload dictated text for this cleanup.

Saved transcripts and local settings

Speakeasy stages completed text privately on the phone before attempting
insertion. Text that cannot be inserted remains in Recovered transcripts. Up to
five transcripts can be held. Saved items expire after 24 hours when the store is
next accessed. If a disk write fails, text may remain only in memory, and new
recording is paused until you resolve it; captured audio is also retained until
text staging succeeds or you explicitly discard it. This is private app storage, not a
separate encrypted vault. Insert or discard an item to remove it. Copy places it
on Android's clipboard and keeps the saved item until you insert or discard it.
Other apps and your device's clipboard features may handle text after you copy it.

Provider credentials, settings, and consent choices are stored in private app
storage. Automatic Android backup and device transfer are disabled for app data.
Settings you explicitly export omit the API key and consent choices. Older export
files may contain API keys and should be kept private. An imported legacy file
can restore a key, but it cannot turn on cloud transcription.

Diagnostics and sharing

Speakeasy keeps a local diagnostic log. It does not intentionally log recordings,
transcripts, API keys, or provider response bodies. Device details, timestamps,
technical errors, and app activity may appear in the log. Older versions may have
logged additional information. Review a log before you choose to copy or share
it. Logs are not automatically sent to us. You can clear them in More settings.
The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK and does not sell personal data.

Your choices and deletion

You can withdraw Accessibility consent using Turn off text access in Privacy and
permissions, or disable Speakeasy in Android Accessibility settings. You can
continue to use on-device dictation through the optional Speakeasy keyboard.
Discard saved audio or recovered transcripts and clear logs within the app.
Clearing Speakeasy's
app storage or uninstalling it removes its private settings, downloaded cleanup
model, logs, consent choices, saved audio, and saved transcripts. Copies you made in other
apps, exported files, clipboard services, or cloud providers are controlled
separately and are not removed by uninstalling Speakeasy.

Speakeasy does not require an app account. It is a general productivity tool and
is not directed at children under 13. We may update this policy when the app's data
handling changes. The effective date above identifies this version. A change to
the purpose of sensitive access requires a new disclosure and consent where
applicable. Contact info@aspectstudio.net with privacy questions.
