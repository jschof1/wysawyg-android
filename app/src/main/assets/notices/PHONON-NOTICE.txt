Fermion Research retrained and quantised the encoder's linear weights to a
five-value alphabet and quantised remaining parameters to 6-bit integer tables.
eschmidbauer converted the model into the phonon-2-c weights.bin,
weights.json and tokens.txt format. WYSAWYG bundles those converted assets
unchanged, uncompressed in the APK; there is no model download on the phone.
